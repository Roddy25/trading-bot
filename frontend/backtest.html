Reading a backtest honestly

Backtests are a research tool, not a forecast. Keep these limitations in mind.

Survivorship & curve fitting

Tuning parameters until a past equity curve looks good is the most common way to build a strategy that fails live. Prefer parameters that work across instruments and periods.

Costs are excluded

These results are gross. Real trading adds spread, commission, swap/financing and slippage — which is why a live account usually underperforms the same backtest.

Daily bars only

Intrabar paths are unknown, so stops and targets are approximated. Intraday strategies such as scalping and arbitrage are poorly represented by daily data.
