What Is Tradify?

Tradify is an AI-powered platform for automated trading. Connect a broker, choose a strategy, and run it in minutes — no coding, no sign-up, and no advanced market knowledge required. Trade anywhere, on mobile or on the web.

Connect your broker

Pick from major brokers and walk through a guided connection flow. Your account snapshot — balance and positions — loads straight into the dashboard.

Advanced control

Prefer more control? Tune every parameter manually — risk per trade, position size, stop-loss, take-profit and max drawdown.

24/5 automation

Once launched, your strategy runs 24/5, executing your automated plan without interruptions.

How Tradify Works

Step 1

Connect Your Broker

Open the app and connect a supported broker through the guided flow. No account, no sign-up — you're ready to launch a strategy in minutes.

Step 2

Choose Your Strategy

Pick from trend following, mean reversion, breakout, grid, DCA, scalping, momentum or arbitrage — each with its own risk profile.

Step 3

Set Your Parameters

Use an AI Preset that matches your risk level, or configure risk per trade, position size, stop-loss, take-profit and max drawdown manually.

Step 4

Monitor & Iterate Your Strategies

Track performance in real time on the dashboard — active strategies, open positions, order history, P&L and a live activity feed — and refine your approach over time.
