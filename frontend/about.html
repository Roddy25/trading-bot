Our mission

Tradify exists to remove the barriers between everyday traders and disciplined, automated execution. For too long, algorithmic trading has been the preserve of those who can code, afford infrastructure, and monitor markets around the clock. We built Tradify so that anyone can launch a fully automated strategy in minutes — no coding, no advanced market knowledge, no constant screen-watching.

What we do

Tradify connects to your existing broker account and manages trade orders according to the strategy you choose. Your funds always remain on your broker account — Tradify never performs deposits, withdrawals, or transfers.

  • AI Presets that match base settings to your chosen risk level
  • A strategy library spanning trend, mean reversion, breakout, grid, DCA, scalping, momentum and arbitrage
  • A built-in VPS so strategies run 24/5 even when your devices are offline
  • Real-time analytics with TradingView integration on web and mobile
  • Seamless multi-broker management from a single interface

Why traders choose Tradify

Automated trading removes emotion from the equation. No greed, no FOMO — just disciplined, rule-based execution that runs whether you are at your desk or asleep. Tradify bots work like a team of professional auto traders, handling entries, exits, and risk management while you focus on improving your strategy.

About this demonstration

This site is a front-end demonstration of the Tradify product experience. Broker connections, account balances, positions, orders and P&L are simulated entirely in your browser using realistic mock data. No real broker account is contacted, no credentials are collected or transmitted, and no real orders are placed. A production version of this product would require broker API credentials, a secure backend, OAuth, and financial regulatory compliance including licensing and KYC/AML.
